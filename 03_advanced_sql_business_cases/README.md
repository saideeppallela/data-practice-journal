## 🔄 Problem-Solving Workflow

My approach to each business case follows a structured workflow:

1. **Understand the problem** — identify the business requirement.
2. **Examine the data** — understand the relevant tables and columns.
3. **Design the solution** — choose suitable SQL techniques.
4. **Write the query** — develop a structured solution.
5. **Validate the result** — check calculations, joins, and edge cases.
6. **Interpret the output** — understand what the results reveal.
7. **Document the solution** — record the approach and relevant findings.
