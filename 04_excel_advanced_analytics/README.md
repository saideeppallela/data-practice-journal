## 📖 Overview

This section focuses on **advanced Microsoft Excel analytics** for practical business data analysis.

The practice roadmap covers dynamic array functions, advanced date calculations, Power Query transformations, What-If Analysis, business reporting, and analytical reports.

The goal is to understand how Excel can be used to transform data, analyze business performance, and communicate findings clearly.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Use dynamic array functions to analyze data efficiently.
- Apply advanced date functions to reporting problems.
- Transform and prepare datasets using Power Query.
- Explore business scenarios using What-If Analysis.
- Build structured business reports and dashboards.
- Analyze business performance and communicate findings.
- Organize projects using a consistent, numbered folder structure.

## 🧠 Key Learning Areas

### 1. Dynamic Array Functions
Explore modern Excel functions for filtering, sorting, selecting, and combining data.

Functions may include:
- `FILTER`
- `SORT`
- `SORTBY`
- `UNIQUE`
- `TAKE`
- `VSTACK`

### 2. Advanced Date Functions
Apply date calculations to support business reporting and time-based analysis.

Functions may include:
- `EDATE`
- `EOMONTH`
- `NETWORKDAYS`
- `WORKDAY`

### 3. Power Query
Practice preparing and transforming datasets through repeatable data preparation workflows.

Key operations include:
- Merge Queries
- Append Queries
- Group By
- Custom Columns
- Data transformation and cleaning

### 4. What-If Analysis
Explore different assumptions and business scenarios to understand their potential impact on results.

### 5. Business Reporting
Organize analysis into structured reports that help communicate business performance and findings.

## 🚀 Learning Approach

Each practice topic follows a problem-solving approach:

1. Understand the business question.
2. Inspect the available data.
3. Select the appropriate Excel feature.
4. Perform the analysis or transformation.
5. Validate the results.
6. Present the findings clearly.

**Goal:** Develop practical advanced Excel skills through organized exercises focused on data transformation, analytical problem-solving, and business reporting.
